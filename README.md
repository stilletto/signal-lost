# Signal Lost

A mobile-first FPV combat-drone simulator. [Play in your browser](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/), [download Android v245 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost.apk), or [download HEAVY v245 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost-heavy-fps.apk).

The existing soldier helmet lights continuously across its curved joins, retaining its geometry, material families, rig and hard edges. Exposed dugout roof timbers now have round closed ends and lengthwise wood grain through the same timber surface and visible-mesh collision. Logs add 140 triangles per position without another material or submission. Trench cuts, floors, earth, accepted assets and all combatants remain unchanged. This batch makes no FPS improvement claim.

The regular ARM64 APK opens the mission menu. HEAVY opens The Third Knock immediately with three aircraft, with reserves following at 15 / 60 metres. Both retain their package IDs, signing certificate and career format; neither bakes in a fresh-profile reset, synthetic pilot, timed exit or free operator purchase. Both have the existing drone artwork and FPS display. Normal / heavy downloads are 90.1 / 90.1 MiB.

Web runs the ordinary menu, fitting and live FPV flow. A disposable local harness using the same exported JS/WASM/PCK physically completes the three-aircraft combat, reserve takeover and dugout entry with three stars. The exported Android game resources also complete that route on desktop Compatibility. Android engine/device acceptance remains separate: the shared Samsung was neither installed nor launched in this batch, and stable handset 30 FPS remains open. Heavy geometry still exceeds the mobile budget. The previously recorded desktop-Vulkan mobile-texture atlas limitation is not a clean runtime pass. Local visual/route captures use the existing 30 FPS cap around shared GPU work; their timings do not establish headroom or handset 30 FPS. In-app-browser GPU timing is unavailable; capped intervals do not certify device performance.

The compiled single-thread Web build and both debug APKs come from source 91733b4ec3e2511ac7f78081ea7ec2aeea470627, produced with Godot 4.7.1. The 739 frozen source/test/tool/project and named asset inputs match the full 1,864-test and 60-scenario smoke gates. build.json identifies the downloadable hashes and runtime/device boundaries. [Source and development evidence](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source).

Third-party art comes from Kenney, Quaternius and Poly Haven; geographic features from OpenStreetMap contributors and NASA SRTM. Gun recordings come from the CC0 Free Firearm Sound Library and credited public-domain DVIDS footage; [sources, edits and limitations](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source/blob/91733b4ec3e2511ac7f78081ea7ec2aeea470627/assets/audio/reports/README.md).
