# Signal Lost

A mobile-first FPV combat-drone simulator. [Play in your browser](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/), [download Android v243 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost.apk), or [download HEAVY v243 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost-heavy-fps.apk).

Walking and running riflemen/gunners now carry their long weapons with both hands, with a smooth low-ready transition and a free arm for casualty dragging. Shouldered fire retains a smaller authored recoil. The same fitted rig now shares one cached visible body with four material families, retaining its geometry, UVs, imported LODs, thermal paint and every combatant. Source Mobile heavy-route submissions fell from 278 to about 208 p95 through the shared skin; moving carry adds about 0.25 ms median CPU for 24 actors on this PC. These component measurements do not establish handset FPS.

The regular ARM64 APK opens the mission menu. HEAVY opens The Third Knock immediately with three aircraft, with reserves following at 15 / 60 metres. Both retain their package IDs, signing certificate and career format; neither bakes in a fresh-profile reset, synthetic pilot, timed exit or free operator purchase. Both have the existing drone artwork and FPS display. Normal / heavy downloads are 90.1 / 90.1 MiB.

Web runs the ordinary menu, fitting and live FPV flow. A disposable local harness using the same exported JS/WASM/PCK physically completes the three-aircraft combat, reserve takeover and dugout entry with three stars. The exported Android game resources also complete that route on desktop Compatibility. Android engine/device acceptance remains separate: the shared Samsung was neither installed nor launched in this batch, and stable handset 30 FPS remains open. Heavy geometry still exceeds the mobile budget. The previously recorded desktop-Vulkan mobile-texture atlas limitation is not a clean runtime pass. Local visual/route captures use the existing 30 FPS cap around shared GPU work; their timings do not establish headroom or handset 30 FPS.

The compiled single-thread Web build and both debug APKs come from source c1483f58c0437cc526c1effe12a9719bdcdb8e5e, produced with Godot 4.7.1. The 733 frozen source/test/tool/project and named asset inputs match the full 1,852-test and 60-scenario smoke gates. build.json identifies the downloadable hashes and runtime/device boundaries. [Source and development evidence](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source).

Third-party art comes from Kenney, Quaternius and Poly Haven; geographic features from OpenStreetMap contributors and NASA SRTM. Gun recordings come from the CC0 Free Firearm Sound Library and credited public-domain DVIDS footage; [sources, edits and limitations](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source/blob/c1483f58c0437cc526c1effe12a9719bdcdb8e5e/assets/audio/reports/README.md).
