# Signal Lost

A mobile-first FPV combat-drone simulator. [Play in your browser](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/), [download Android v244 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost.apk), or [download HEAVY v244 with FPS](https://stilletto.github.io/signal-lost/signal-lost-heavy-fps.apk).

The mission menu keeps the full operation name, career and next-rank goal readable in English and Russian, through the existing ledger layout. The buried dugout has a faceted soil crown; its collider follows the visible soil and timber instead of an invisible box up to 1.12 m above them. The unchanged excavation, trench floors, support, materials and combatants remain canonical. The crown adds 48 triangles per position without an extra rendering surface. This batch makes no FPS improvement claim.

The regular ARM64 APK opens the mission menu. HEAVY opens The Third Knock immediately with three aircraft, with reserves following at 15 / 60 metres. Both retain their package IDs, signing certificate and career format; neither bakes in a fresh-profile reset, synthetic pilot, timed exit or free operator purchase. Both have the existing drone artwork and FPS display. Normal / heavy downloads are 90.1 / 90.1 MiB.

Web runs the ordinary menu, fitting and live FPV flow. A disposable local harness using the same exported JS/WASM/PCK physically completes the three-aircraft combat, reserve takeover and dugout entry with three stars. The exported Android game resources also complete that route on desktop Compatibility. Android engine/device acceptance remains separate: the shared Samsung was neither installed nor launched in this batch, and stable handset 30 FPS remains open. Heavy geometry still exceeds the mobile budget. The previously recorded desktop-Vulkan mobile-texture atlas limitation is not a clean runtime pass. Local visual/route captures use the existing 30 FPS cap around shared GPU work; their timings do not establish headroom or handset 30 FPS. In-app-browser GPU timing is unavailable; capped intervals do not certify device performance.

The compiled single-thread Web build and both debug APKs come from source c3523b185ae0101bdc63f1c2a56f0f1d19bc87a5, produced with Godot 4.7.1. The 733 frozen source/test/tool/project and named asset inputs match the full 1,854-test and 60-scenario smoke gates. build.json identifies the downloadable hashes and runtime/device boundaries. [Source and development evidence](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source).

Third-party art comes from Kenney, Quaternius and Poly Haven; geographic features from OpenStreetMap contributors and NASA SRTM. Gun recordings come from the CC0 Free Firearm Sound Library and credited public-domain DVIDS footage; [sources, edits and limitations](https://github.com/stilletto/signal-lost-source/blob/c3523b185ae0101bdc63f1c2a56f0f1d19bc87a5/assets/audio/reports/README.md).
